# SMB AI Readiness Scorecard

**A free 5-minute assessment that shows a small business where it stands on AI and what to do first.**

👉 **[Take the assessment](https://alealonso-ai.github.io/ai-readiness-scorecard/)**

---

## Why this exists

Small and mid-sized businesses hear "you need AI" everywhere, but most of the advice is written for large enterprises or built to sell something. Owners are left asking: *Are we ready? Where do we even start?*

This scorecard gives a straight, vendor-neutral answer in five minutes, with no sign-up.

## How it works

1. Answer 18 multiple-choice questions across six areas:
   - **Strategy & Leadership**: does the business know what it wants from AI?
   - **Data & Information**: can you get to your information quickly?
   - **Processes**: is the work documented and repeatable?
   - **Tools & Technology**: are you on modern, cloud-based tools?
   - **People & Skills**: is the team open to AI and trained?
   - **Governance & Risk**: are there rules to use AI safely?
2. Get an overall readiness score (0–100) and a level: **Explorer, Experimenter, Builder or Leader**.
3. See a score for each area and your **top 3 next steps**, drawn from your lowest-scoring areas.
4. Save the results as a PDF.

**Privacy:** everything runs in your browser. No answers are collected, stored or sent anywhere.

## Scoring method

- Each answer is worth 0–3 points.
- Each area's score is its points as a percentage of the maximum (9).
- The overall score is the average of the six areas.
- Recommendations come from the three weakest areas. Areas under 50% get a foundation step; areas at 50% or above get a next-level step.

## Tech

A single `index.html` file (HTML, CSS and plain JavaScript) with no frameworks or dependencies, hosted for free on GitHub Pages.

To customize the questions or recommendations, edit the `AREAS` list in the **CONTENT** section of `index.html`.
